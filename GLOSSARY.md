# Glossary

Domain vocabulary for `ledger-core`.

## Terms

- **Account**: An identified entity categorised under Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Income, or Expenses.
- **Posting**: A single credit or debit entry with an amount and an associated Account.
- **Transaction**: A balanced set of postings where the sum of debits equals the sum of credits.
- **Balance**: The accumulated sum of postings for an account at a given point in time.
- **Net Worth**: Total Assets minus total Liabilities.
- **P&L (Profit and Loss)**: Income minus Expenses over a specific period.
